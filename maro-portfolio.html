Astronomy research

gravitational microlensing

When a star passes in front of a more distant one, its gravity bends and magnifies the background light. A planet around the lens star can add a short spike to that brightening, sometimes only hours long.

In my research internship I looked for the combinations of parameters that make planets in the habitable zone easier to detect this way.

  • Modeled light curves in Python with MulensModel.
  • Varied mass ratio q, projected separation s and Einstein crossing time tE.
  • Processed synthetic and observed datasets and mapped detectability across parameter space.
  • Presented results at international research meetings.
